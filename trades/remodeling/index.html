Remodeling details

One space. Many trades.

A remodel is several trades sharing one space, often with people still living or working in it. Demo, rough-in, drywall and finishes follow each other, and a crew that arrives in the wrong phase is a crew waiting around. Say which phase the request is for, who else is on site and how the space is being used.

What to include in your request

  1. 01

    The phase

    Demo, rough-in, drywall, finishes or punch list.

  2. 02

    Rooms and scope

    Kitchen, bath, basement or a whole floor, and what stays as is.

  3. 03

    Trades on site

    Who else is working, and when, so the crew fits the sequence.

  4. 04

    Occupancy

    Whether people live or work there during the job, and any quiet hours.

  5. 05

    Protection and cleanup

    Floors and furniture to protect, dust control, and where debris goes.

Put these in the job details. Estimates are fine.

How requests are reviewed

The phase, the rooms and the trades already on site get read together, so the request fits the sequence you're running. It's a request, not a booking: no crew is confirmed from the form.

FAQ

Remodeling questions.

01Should I mention if the space is occupied?

Yes. If people live or work there during the remodel, say so. It affects working hours, dust control and how the crew moves through the building.

02How do I describe a remodel with several phases?

List the phases in order (for example demo, rough-in, drywall, finishes) and mark which one this request is for. Note the trades already on site, so the crew fits around them.

03Should I list what has to stay protected?

Yes. List the floors, cabinets, fixtures or furniture that stay in place, plus any dust or noise limits set by the owner or the building.

04Should I mention permits and inspections?

Yes, if they set the pace. Note which inspections are coming, like rough-in, so the request shows where the job needs to be and by when.

05A remodel needs several trades. Do I send one request?

Each request has one trade. Pick Remodeling, or the trade the phase needs most, and list the rest in the job details.
