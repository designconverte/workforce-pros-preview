Hardscape details

It starts under the pavers.

Hardscape is only as good as what's under it. Before the first paver or stone goes down, there's excavation, base gravel, compaction and a slope that sends water away from the building. That's why the area alone doesn't describe a hardscape job: the material, the base, the drainage and the access all belong in the request. Retaining walls add their own math: height, block type and drainage behind the wall.

What to include in your request

  1. 01

    Material

    Concrete pavers, natural stone, brick or wall block.

  2. 02

    Area

    Square feet of patio, walkway or driveway, and linear feet of any wall.

  3. 03

    Base and drainage

    Excavation depth, gravel and sand, slope, and where the water should go.

  4. 04

    Removal

    Whether an old surface, sod or soil comes out first.

  5. 05

    Access

    Gates, side yards and driveways, and room for equipment and pallets.

Put these in the job details. Estimates are fine.

How requests are reviewed

Area, material and base get read together, and the access you describe shows how much of the job is hand work. It's a request, not a booking: no crew is confirmed from the form.

FAQ

Hardscape questions.

01What details help with a paver or patio request?

The area in square feet, the material, whether the old surface comes out first, and how the base is planned: excavation depth, gravel and sand. If there's a drainage plan or a grade to follow, mention it.

02Does site access matter for a hardscape request?

Yes. Say how equipment and materials reach the work area: a wide gate or a narrow side yard, a driveway or a lawn to cross. Access affects how much of the job is done by hand.

03Should I mention the weather window?

Yes. Base work and pavers need workable ground, so note whether the start date is fixed or can move with the weather.

04Should I mention utilities or irrigation lines?

Yes. Note whether utilities have been marked through 811 and where irrigation or drain lines run. Digging plans change around them.

05Who can send a hardscape request?

Companies. Workforce Pros rents construction labor to businesses, so the request asks for your company name, the area and the material.
