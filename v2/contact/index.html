FAQ

Straight answers.

01Who can request a crew?

Restoration companies that need more hands on a job. Workforce Pros supplies labor; it doesn't take on restoration jobs itself. The request asks for a company name. Working on your own? Use your business name.

02What details should I have ready?

The job site ZIP, the labor you need, about how many people and when you'd like to start. Job details are optional but useful: the type of loss and the phase of the job, days or weeks, daily hours, residential or commercial, and the gear the job calls for.

03Does a request confirm a crew?

No. A request tells us what the job needs; it isn't a booking. No crew, start date or rate is set by the form.
