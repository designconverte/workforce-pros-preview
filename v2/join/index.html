FAQ

Straight answers.

01Which roles can I apply for?

Helper, painter, drywall, carpenter, finisher and supervisor. If yours isn't listed, choose Other.

02Can I apply in Spanish?

Yes. The application is in English and in Spanish.

03What do I need to apply?

Your role, your full name and a phone number. Text messages are optional.

04Can I choose more than one role?

The application takes one. Pick the one you do most.

05Does applying mean I have a job?

No. Applying sends your role and contact details to Workforce Pros. It isn't a job offer or a placement.

06Do I have to agree to text messages?

No. Text messages are optional and aren't a condition of applying.
