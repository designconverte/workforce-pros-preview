FAQ

Straight answers.

01Who can request a crew?

Restoration companies that need more hands on a job. Workforce Pros supplies labor; it doesn't take on restoration jobs itself. The request asks for a company name. Working on your own? Use your business name.

02What kind of labor can I request?

Three kinds: general labor (helpers), skilled labor (painting, drywall, carpentry and finishing) and supervisors. Each one has its own page with what to put in the request. Need something else? Pick Other and describe the role.

03What details should I have ready?

The job site ZIP, the labor you need, about how many people and when you'd like to start. Job details are optional but useful: the type of loss and the phase of the job, days or weeks, daily hours, residential or commercial, and the gear the job calls for.

04What if I'm not sure about the crew size or start date?

Pick Not sure yet for either one and add what you do know in the job details. A rough request is a fine place to start.

05Can I request more than one kind of labor?

Each request has one main pick. Choose the one that leads the work and list the rest in the job details.

06What happens after I send a request?

You'll see a confirmation that it was received. Then we review the labor, crew size, start date and job site you sent.

07Does a request confirm a crew?

No. A request tells us what the job needs; it isn't a booking. No crew, start date or rate is set by the form.
